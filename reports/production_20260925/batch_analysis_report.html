Most of this set is pre-annotation that a human reviewed, not annotation a human created: 12,640 of 12,793 boxes arrived with an imported file. That is worth knowing before treating it as ground truth.

The boxes are genuinely rotated. 11,600 of the 12,135 rotated boxes, 95.6 percent, sit at an angle that is not a multiple of 90 degrees, and the values spread across all eight 45 degree buckets, so any pipeline that assumes axis-aligned rectangles will mis-draw almost all of them.

Measured against the cavity of each image's own part rather than the image centre, because the part is not centred in the frame, the median box centre sits at 0.90 of the cavity radius, the 95th percentile at 1.18 and the maximum at 1.54. Only 3 boxes of 12,793 fall further out than 1.5 times the radius, so nothing is annotated on the tray or the background. The median offset of the part from the image centre is 100 px and the maximum is 450 px, so any position statistic measured from the image centre is wrong.

10 · What to do with it

Ordered actions, each with the number that makes it necessary.

  1. Train on the 20 base codes and keep all 24 in the catalogue. The four _MICRON_THRESHOLD codes are a deterministic function of the base code's box size, so feeding them in as independent targets hands the model labels that come from box geometry and builds guaranteed correlations into the label matrix that will flatter the metrics. Use the gate as a sampling flag for the edge training set instead. This is a recommendation and it has not been decided.
  2. Confirm Stretched Bubble against the client legend, and ask what validation did to Dirty Camera. The four codes named on 2026-10-13 are Dirty Camera on 204 images, Bubble Irregular on 108, Folded Lens on 59 and Stretched Bubble on 2; the first three match the September code legend directly, and only Stretched Bubble rests on elimination. Dirty Camera also moved from 28 to 204 between the email and the delivered file, and that jump is not explained.
  3. Ask the annotator which reading of a box's size the threshold script uses, and whether the micron tags were hand-edited during validation. The email states 647 micrometres but not how size is measured, no reading of size reproduces all four codes, and the flag counts moved between the email and the delivered file.
  4. Settle P2_FOV against F2_FOV and get the annotator's definition of P3_HE. The two files disagree on the prefix for View Obstructed, and P3_HE's boxes have a median diagonal of 22,149 micrometres against a 14,240 micrometre lens, so it marks a region whose coverage is not recorded anywhere.
  5. Decide the multi-label training scheme. 743 of 1,047 images carry more than one code, so a single-folder-per-class split would put most of the set in the Bubble bin and discard the co-occurring labels. Also note that 19 images carry two size-gate flags and one carries three, so the edge-training selection the email describes is not a partition.
  6. Decide whether the 9,391 unannotated images in the source folder are wanted as explicit negatives. This batch is 1,047 of the 10,438 images in that folder.
  7. Look at the over-200 px boxes before training. 52 of 10,075 P2_BB boxes and 63 of 591 P2_WP boxes exceed 200 px across while their class medians are 9.2 px and 45.7 px, so they are likely region marks or mislabels rather than defects at that scale.
