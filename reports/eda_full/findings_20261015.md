# Full-coverage EDA findings

What the three ICube library folders hold, how the labels resolve, how large each
defect class is, what the client's own size gate implies, and where the sources
disagree. The workbook holds the detail, the report holds the navigable version,
and this page holds the findings.

## The set

One EDA across every picture in the three library folders, plus the production
image folder, with every label resolved under the agreed priority rule and keyed
on content hash, never on a path. Four artefacts: the multi-sheet workbook, the
HTML report, the self-contained distributions widget, and this findings page. The
workbook carries a coverage sheet and a crosswalk sheet as well.

## Headlines

- **10,614 distinct pictures** across the three library folders, **12,079 files**,
  and **13,126 files** once the production image folder is added. The production
  folder adds no new picture: all 1,047 of its files resolve by content hash to a
  picture already in a library folder.
- **The two annotation readings are different numbers and are never swapped.**
  6,690 pictures are MENTIONED by some file, but only **4,782 carry a shape**,
  **5,832 carry no shape**, and **3,924 are mentioned nowhere**. Each pair sums to
  the census: 4,782 plus 5,832, and 6,690 plus 3,924, both give 10,614.
- **20,014 resolved shapes: 14,182 boxes and 5,832 tags.** The 5,832 no-shape
  picture count and the 5,832 tag-shape count are the same number seen twice,
  which is why every table names which one it means.
- **28 canonical defects**, with two code collisions kept unmerged and flagged
  rather than silently combined: the shared code that means HEMA Fragment in one
  project and HEMA Obstruction in the production files, and the view-obstructed
  defect written with two different prefixes.
- **14,182 boxes carry across 1,130 distinct pictures.** Only **4.65 percent** of
  the boxes are axis-aligned, so any conversion that assumes upright rectangles
  mis-draws the other 95 percent.
- **The cavity was measured on 10,470 of 10,614 pictures.** The 144 that could not
  be measured are one named class, frames too dark for any bright blob to be
  found, counted rather than dropped.
- **The customer's size gate is a clean cut on the LONGER SIDE of the annotation
  box**, not the diagonal: zero images fall on the wrong side of the boundary,
  against **39 that the diagonal misfiles** on foreign matter and 4 on fiber.

## The adopted scale

- **The adopted working scale is 15.337 microns per pixel.** It is derived from
  the customer's own documented 300 micron cut: the micron-threshold tags
  switch at a defect longer side of **19.41 to 19.61 px**, and 300 divided by
  the 19.51 px midpoint gives the scale, matching the frozen central figure of
  15.3369 with an interval of 15.298 to 15.376. Every micron figure in these
  deliverables is a pixel figure times 15.337.
- **The per-line values reproduce it.** The same inversion reads 15.377 on L24,
  15.601 on L25 (only 14 pictures, loose), 15.400 on L26 and 15.337 on L27.
  L31 has no threshold defect in the production export and is not computable.
  Per folder group it reads 15.377 on the updated folders and 15.400 on
  pictures that are also in the old defects library.
- **The old 647 micron cut inverts to the same scale**, from a boundary at
  42.08 to 42.79 px, and is the independent reproduction.
- **The prior scales 18.1165 and 18.25 are superseded.** 18.1165 divided an
  unsourced 14,200 micron lens assumption by a lens pixel measurement; 18.25 is
  written in no document we hold. Both appear only in columns and sentences
  explicitly labelled as superseded.
- **The lens rim measures 789 px** (interquartile range 764.4 to 809.5 px),
  about 12.10 mm at the adopted scale. The earlier 848 px lens figure is
  disproven: it was a least-squares circle fit spanning two rings, and
  restricted to the rim band the same fit gives 789.0 px.
- **Limitation, stated plainly and not resolved here: the lens nominal
  diameter is not documented in anything received.** The only documented ruler
  is the customer's own 300 micron cut. A written lens diameter from the
  customer would close the remaining question.
- **At the adopted scale the gate boundary reads 298 to 301 microns**,
  reproducing the customer's own 300 micron cut.
- **No production line and no folder group differs from another beyond the
  measurement noise.** The within-stratum spread of 10 to 26 percent dwarfs the
  spread among the candidate scales, so measurement alone cannot settle a scale
  question of that size.
- **The widely quoted 113 folder-disagreement figure is a name-based count.** On
  content identity the overlap is larger, not smaller: **279 pictures library-wide
  and 129 on the production batch**, because a name-based route cannot see a
  picture that was renamed.
- **One production picture is annotated twice**, under two byte-identical file
  names that both carry the same eight shapes, so the 1,047 production files hold
  1,046 distinct contents.
- **Coverage of the production blob folder is 1,047 annotated of 10,438 pictures,
  10.03 percent.** The remainder is available as negatives and was not pulled.

## What each part is

- **Census.** Ten thousand six hundred and fourteen distinct pictures sit across
  12,079 library files; the same picture can be stored under more than one name,
  so 187 file names hide a duplicate content and a path-based count undercounts.
- **Labels.** The labels resolve to 28 canonical defects. The set is multi-label:
  the median picture carries one code and the mean is 1.68, with a thin tail that
  reaches 11 codes on the busiest picture.
- **Box geometry.** Box size is the rectangle diagonal, invariant to rotation, and
  every micron figure names the scale it was computed at. Two classes are region
  annotations rather than defect spots: their boxes span more than the whole lens,
  so they must not be read as detector targets.
- **Blind scale.** The lens is the same physical part on every line, so the
  measured rim in pixels is a direct read on the scale. Between strata the rim
  median spans 688 to 763 px, a 10.2 percent range, and the within-stratum spread
  is 10 to 26 percent, so nothing stands apart.
- **Micron gate.** The gate is the customer's own authority; everything derived from
  it is reported beside it, never instead of it. It is a clean size cut on the
  longer side of the box, and inverting it gives the adopted scale above.
- **Coverage and disagreement.** Every stratum names the population it is measured
  in. The folder disagreement is reported under both a name-based rule and a
  content-identity rule, with which is which stated.
- **Crosswalk.** Every code in every source maps to exactly one canonical defect,
  frozen, with the two collisions flagged.

## What is superseded

The 2026-10-13 production-batch analysis of 1,047 pictures is superseded. It
analysed one batch and reported 12,793 boxes and 1,318 tags over 24 codes. This
analysis covers 10,614 pictures and resolves 20,014 shapes over 28 canonical
defects, a much larger set. The superseded record is kept in its own labelled
section of the report, with the delta stated, and is not silently overwritten.

## What to do

1. **Ask the customer for a written lens diameter.** The adopted scale of 15.337
   microns per pixel rests on the customer's own documented 300 micron cut,
   which is the only documented ruler received. The lens nominal is
   undocumented, so a written lens diameter from the customer would close the
   remaining scale question.
2. **Ask which reading of a box's size the threshold script uses.** The data
   supports the longer side of the box over the diagonal, but the proxy itself is
   our inference, not a written rule.
3. **Settle the two code collisions.** Each decides whether a pair of defects
   merges, which moves every count that carries them.
4. **Decide the multi-label training scheme.** A single picture can carry up to 11
   codes, so a one-folder-per-class split discards most of what the annotators
   wrote.
5. **Decide whether the unannotated pictures are wanted as explicit negatives.**
   Coverage of the blob folder is 10.03 percent, so the remainder is available at
   no labelling cost.
6. **Treat the region-annotation classes as regions, not detector targets.** Their
   boxes span more than the whole lens, so a detector target read would be wrong
   at that scale.
