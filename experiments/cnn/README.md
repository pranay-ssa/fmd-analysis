## Warmup: what it does and does not change

- The warmup block runs two dummy passes plus one throwaway pass on the real test pipeline, then the timed pass. All of them are discarded.
- **Accuracy, per-class recall/precision/F1, and the confusion matrix are warmup-independent.** Inference does not update weights and the predictions are identical; this is also recorded in `reports/cnn/OOI_CNN_Analysis_CORRECTED_20260908.md` section 6.3 ("Accuracy, F1, and training time (unchanged by warmup)").
- **Only `inference_per_image_ms` depends on warmup.** Without it, a one-time process cost (XLA graph re-trace plus cuDNN autotune) lands inside the timed window and is then divided by the test-set size, which inflates ms/img.
