# Week 48

## 23 November

**Asked:** show the standing tasks, then hold the lead questions and start housekeeping. Take the
annotation export out of git, drop the Notion board as the working habit, update the handoff.

**Answered by the lead, and recorded in `docs/decisions/2026-11-23-fm-debris-scope.md`:**

- "FM and Debris" means the four size-tagged classes: Foreign Matter, Fiber, Extraneous Polymer, HEMA.
- Strict PASS allows an untagged object of another debris class. A picture passes when none of the four
  carries a size tag, as long as it also has no presentation tag, no Bubble Cluster and no Wet Package.

**Recounted on the frozen split, because the lead expected the case to be absent from the data.** It is
not absent. 755 of 13,631 pictures carry one of the four classes with no size flag at all, and 244 of
them pass under the strict rule (175 train, 37 validation, 32 test). Untagged instances per class:
Foreign Matter 966, Fiber 90, Extraneous Polymer 4, HEMA 0. Two counts were re-derived by a second,
independent method (an awk pass over the CSVs) and matched.

**New edge case raised, not decided:** 18 of the 244 carry no Bubble box at all and still pass, because
nothing on them fails. Whether a Bubble-free object set should pass needs one sentence in the rule.

Bubble boxes and no tags, which passes today. All 12 test pictures are unaffected.
