# Week 46

## What was asked

On the evening of 5 November the lead opened the next phase: two experiments, both followed by a
conditional pass/fail block.

1. fifteen lens-presentation tags plus eight object-of-interest tags in one multi-label CNN;
2. fifteen lens-presentation tags plus one object-of-interest tag, with the object classes redirected
   to a detector.

She could not meet the following morning and asked the team to settle the remaining choices among
themselves. Two questions went back to her: in phase 2, does the detector carry the object classes or
only a coarse tag, and which detector version. The week-45 deck is with her for final formatting.

## What was done on 6 November

- **Phase 1 status corrected before it was repeated.** The nineteen-column head is trained (twelve lens
  plus seven object classes, inverse-frequency weighting, 4 November) and the pass/fail rule was
  measured, but only as workbook arithmetic. The lead's "fifteen plus eight" is the guide count; the
  data carries twelve and seven, because four classes have no pictures. That reconciliation is written
  down rather than left in a chat reply.
- **The conditional block is now a module.** `experiments/multilabel_ooi/pass_fail_block.py` implements
  the decided rule once, with two interchangeable object sources: the CNN's object columns for phase 1
  and a detector's class list for phase 2. It returns the verdict with attribution, and it rejects an
  unknown detector label instead of dropping it. Ten rule self-tests pass.
- **The block is wired into the pipeline and proven on pictures, not just on saved arrays.** The same
  module that scores the test split also scores a picture from disk: resize, normalise, forward, sigmoid,
  decide. Sixteen test pictures rescored on the VM came back bit-identical to the saved scores and the
  other twelve within 0.0005, with no verdict disagreement. What remains on the phase-1 side is a
  capture-side runner, not a modelling question.
- **The block was extended with a per-class tally** (`--per-class`), because "which class causes the
  fail" is the question a reviewer asks and the four headline numbers do not answer it. At 0.5, per
  class: Foreign Matter 110 of 112 caught and 23 good pictures wrongly failed, Fiber 57 of 58 caught
  and 11 wrongly failed, Low Dose 102 of 102 with 6 wrongly failed, Wet Package 47 of 47 with 5. All
  29 false fails sit on 1 Clear and 28 bubble-only pictures, which is the same specificity problem
  already on record.
- **The real-time path is built and measured.** `predict_picture.py` loads the head once and takes a
  picture path to a verdict in one call. Single picture at a time on the VM: 29.2 ms median on GPU
  (decode 21.7, forward 7.5, rule 0.03) and 53.9 ms on CPU, against a 200 ms budget. Cold start, import
  plus checkpoint load, is 3.56 s, which is why the line keeps the process resident rather than
  starting one per picture.
- **The pipeline is one entry point, not one script.** `run_arm.py` chains the stages for an arm:
  decide the saved scores, write `decision.json`, and merge a `decision` section into the arm's
  `run.json` without touching metrics, then read the file back to prove nothing was lost. The block
  stays out of the trainer, because it has to run where torch is not installed and because phase 2
  feeds the same rule from a detector. Deciding a lens-only arm is refused, since it would read all
  120 object-only test pictures as clear.
- **The two questions are answered with evidence** in
  `docs/wayfinder/next_phase_two_arms_20261106.md`: the detector carries the seven classes, because the
  pass rule passes bubble-only pictures and a coarse tag cannot tell a bubble from contamination (it
  would fail all 36 bubble-only test pictures); and the detector is YOLO26n, the family already in use,
  with the measured crop comparison against YOLO11n recorded together with its single-seed caveat.

## Numbers produced

- Rule verification at 0.5: 760/768 caught, 316/317 clear passed, 8/36 bubble-only passed, 324/353
  passed overall.
- Rule verification at the chosen cuts: 759/768, 316/317, 9/36, 325/353.
- Drivers on failed test pictures at 0.5, top five: Missing Primary Package 212, Multiple Lenses 151,

## 9 November - Experiment 1 workbook, the pass-rule correction, and the Experiment 2 plan

- **The Experiment 1 workbook is built and verified.** Seven sheets and five charts, written for a
  reader who did not run the work: population and split, the seven object classes with prevalence
  floors, the twelve lens conditions, the size flag against all class pictures, both pass rules, and
  the method and definitions. The lead has it for review.
- **The gate comparison is settled for reporting.** All class pictures score 0.4955 macro AUPRC
  against 0.4391 for size-flagged only, and 0.5100 against 0.4828 macro F1, on identical truth and
  recipe. The gate costs ranking quality, not coverage: the gate-trained model still found 0.800 of
  the 40 un-gated Foreign Matter test pictures. Class labels remain the training target; the flag is
  reported as "of which size-flagged".
- **The pass rule is reported two ways.** Rule as run (Clear, or only Bubble and Bubble Cluster):
  760 of 768 failing pictures caught, 1 of 317 clear wrongly failed, 8 of 36 bubble-family passed,
  324 of 353 overall at 0.5. Only Bubble passes: 776 of 785 caught, 1 of 317 clear wrongly failed,
  6 of 19 bubble-only passed, 322 of 336 overall. At the chosen cuts the alternative catches 778 of
  785 and wrongly fails 2 clear pictures. The model scores are identical; only the rule changes.
- **The Cluster decision belongs to the lead.** 1,110 of 1,132 Cluster pictures also carry Bubble,
  but only 47 of those 1,110 are in the pass bucket and 1,063 already fail on another label. The
  rule touches 68 split pictures and 17 test pictures, not the whole Cluster population. The working
  rule keeps Bubble and Clear; Cluster is going to the lead.
- **The pass rule is a working reading, not a client rule.** The 4 November wording "agreed"
  overstated it. The first written source is the assistant's 3 November proposal; the user asked on
  9 November to keep Bubble and Clear for now and to put Cluster to the lead. Ticket 035 and the
  4 November decision record now carry a correction.
- **Experiment 2 is YOLO26, and its dataset is built.** Lens tags stay with the CNN; the seven
  object classes move to a YOLO26n detector, Bubble and Bubble Cluster separate, same frozen split,
  same pass/fail block. The spec is `docs/wayfinder/exp2_yolo_detector_20261109.md`. The dataset is
  built on the VM at `/data/FMD_Data_26082026/fmd_detector_20261110` (7,470 symlinks and labels,
  41 MB) and the verifier exits 0: 1,563 train, 341 validation and 343 test pictures carry boxes;
  3,676, 769 and 778 are empty-label backgrounds. Training is held for the lead's review; the driver
  is ready. The September arena (346/88 pictures, older annotation, two legacy classes) is not a
  phase-2 result.

## 10 November - Experiment 2 spec clarified, first arm trained

- **The two-model architecture is written down.** The CNN and YOLO see the same frame as two
  specialists, not a relay. The CNN supplies the 12 lens scores; YOLO is the only object authority
  for the seven object classes. The CNN's object columns are ignored, not used as a first opinion
  and not confirmed by YOLO. The pass/fail block is the only place the two meet. The spec now says
  this explicitly, with the three rejected alternatives: CNN classifies and YOLO confirms, CNN
  outputs one object tag, and CNN routes only object pictures to YOLO.
- **The GPU was checked before staging.** At 05:58 UTC the A100 had 38,445 MiB in use at 94%:
  srikantht's YOLO training held 35,920 MiB and a streamlit app 2,482 MiB. No pranayp process was on
  the GPU. The GPU freed at 07:33 UTC; the watcher waited 10 minutes and started the all arm at
  07:43 UTC. It finished at 09:51 UTC after 7,760 s (2 h 9 min) and 100 epochs.
- **Two arms.** `all` trains on `data_all.yaml` (primary, all 5,239 training pictures, empty labels
  for the 3,676 object-free ones). `objects` trains on `data_objects.yaml` (comparison, the 1,563
  pictures with boxes). Both use YOLO26n at 1280, batch 8, 100 epochs, seed 42, and write `run.json`
  inside their arm folder. The boxed-only arm started at 10:10 UTC and finished at 10:57 UTC after
  2,815 s; best epoch 82.
- **The all arm result.** Test mAP50-95 0.1707, mAP50 0.3317, precision 0.412, recall 0.383 over
  1,121 pictures and 5,472 boxes. Per class AP50-95: Bubble 0.388 (4,249 boxes), HEMA 0.498 (27),
  Wet Package 0.158 (202), Foreign Matter 0.073 (400), Bubble Cluster 0.051 (493), Fiber 0.023 (83),
  Extraneous Polymer 0.004 (18). Validation best was epoch 96 at 0.1478; test is a different
  population.
- **Pass/fail on the all arm.** Bubble and Cluster pass: 746 of 768 failing pictures caught, 316 of
  317 clear passed, 24 of 36 bubble-family passed, 340 of 353 overall at the chosen cuts; 730 of 768
  and 337 of 353 at 0.5. Only Bubble passes: 751 of 768 caught, 316 of 317 clear, 10 of 26
  bubble-only, 326 of 343 overall at the chosen cuts. The chosen Cluster cut is 0.03, so nearly every
  bubble picture fires Cluster under the only-Bubble rule; that cut objective needs a decision before
  the rule is quoted.
- **The boxed-only result.** Test mAP50-95 0.1475, mAP50 0.2624, precision 0.290, recall 0.424, best
  epoch 82. Per class AP50-95: HEMA 0.589, Bubble 0.187, Wet Package 0.137, Foreign Matter 0.063,
  Bubble Cluster 0.026, Extraneous Polymer 0.022, Fiber 0.008. Against the all arm, precision falls
  and recall rises, and Bubble halves.
- **Pass/fail on the boxed arm.** Bubble and Cluster pass at 0.5: 738 of 768 fails caught, 307 of
  317 clear passed, 328 of 353 overall, against 730, 316 and 337 for the all arm. At the chosen cuts:
  743 of 768, 270 of 317, 293 of 353, against 746, 316 and 340. Only Bubble at the chosen cuts:
  752 of 768, 141 of 317, 153 of 343, against 751, 316 and 326. The boxed arm's chosen Cluster cut,
  0.12, fires on Clear pictures.
- **Reading the comparison.** The 3,676 background pictures are doing specificity work. Removing them
  makes the detector fire on empty frames, and that shows up as Clear false fails: 1 against 47 at
  the chosen Bubble-and-Cluster cuts. The all-pictures arm stays the production choice. The caveat is
  that at 100 epochs the boxed arm takes 196 batches per epoch against 655, so it also has fewer
  optimizer steps; this is the practical recipe comparison, not a pure background ablation.
- **A run-record defect was fixed.** The first `run.json` wrote an error into `metrics.per_class`
  because the driver asked for `AP50`, `Precision` and `Recall`, while ultralytics 8.4 returns
  `Box-P`, `Box-R`, `Box-F1`, `mAP50` and `mAP50-95`. The driver is corrected; the existing record
  was repaired by `vm/ops/repair_detector_run_json.py` and its primary and overall numbers are
  unchanged.
- **The stage record is in the run folder.** `run/detector_20261110/stage_status.json` holds the GPU
  check, the watcher PID, the training times and the result. `run.json` and `test_per_class.csv` are
  in both arm folders, `run/detector_20261110/yolo26n_fmd_7cls_all/` and
  `run/detector_20261110/yolo26n_fmd_7cls_objects/`; the launcher and driver are on the VM under
  `vm/drivers/`.
- **The Experiment 2 workbook is built.** Six sheets, four charts, A3 print setup, PDF preview
  checked and a client-safe token scan clean. It is at
  `reports/multilabel_production/FMD_Experiment2_Detector_20261110.xlsx` and waits for the user's
  review before sharing.
- **The week-46 draft deck is built.** Fifteen slides in `decks/drafts/`, with a native AP chart and
  a PDF review copy. It uses a minimal editorial layout: Cambria titles, Calibri body, numbered
  rails, no accent stripes. The strict rule is shown first; the same 353 pass candidates are then
  compared: only Bubble gives 322 passes and 31 false fails, Bubble + Cluster gives 324 and 29. The
  deck states the catch-count cost and explains why classes beat the size gate, why Cluster is a
  separate class, and why 17 test pictures move. The detector section has design and expected
  questions only, with no detector results.
- **The internal deck style is extracted.** `decks/reference/internal_deck_style.md` and `.json` record
  the 9 November deck's grid, Arial typography, orange title, blue table header, footer placement and
