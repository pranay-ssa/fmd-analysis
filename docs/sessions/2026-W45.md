# Week 45

## What was asked

Split the single object-of-interest flag into the eight guide object classes, answer whether the
model should learn classes or the micron gate, prove the lens numbers survive sharing a head, and
turn the result into a picture-level pass/fail rule reported in a client-safe workbook. Run it as a
wayfinder map on the kanban board, autonomously under pre-registered rules, with the numbers shown
after each round.

## Round by round

**027 label table.** One table over the frozen split (7,470 rows, 27 columns) carrying both column
groups: seven object classes and four gate subsets. 798 Foreign Matter pictures of which 454 are
size-flagged; 798 = 454 + 344 verified as a subset, zero violations. Surface Groove is zero pictures
and stays as a zero column. Verifier exits 0 with three tamper proofs.

**028 trainer.** `experiments/multilabel_ooi/train_multilabel.py`, one script with `--columns
ooi|combined`, `--gated-target class|gate`, `--pos-weight on|off`. Its smoke passed locally and on
the VM after the worker's `sudo` push was refused by the single-query guard; the operator pushed the
trainer and the label table by hand and closed the card.

**029 round 1, class versus gate.** Two runs, same truth, same seed. Class 0.4955 macro AP against
gate 0.4391. The gate costs Foreign Matter, Wet Package and HEMA, and — contrary to the pre-run
expectation — does not cap recall on un-gated objects (0.800 recall on un-gated Foreign Matter).
Fiber reversed (0.315 gate against 0.266 class) on 2 un-gated test pictures; not a finding.

**030 verify round 1.** All 14 APs, both F1 operating points, all cut re-selections, confusion
matrices and recall-by-flag reproduced from predictions. Verdict: class target.

**031 round 2, weighted.** Inverse-frequency weight on the training split. Macro AP 0.6330 (+0.1375),
with HEMA +0.394, Extraneous Polymer +0.319, Wet Package +0.182, Fiber +0.080. Macro F1 at 0.5 rose
from 0.259 to 0.614, which makes the default operating point usable.

**032 verify round 2.** 135 comparisons across three arms, 0 failures. Weight proceeds.

**033 round 3, combined.** Twelve lens plus seven object columns in one head. Attempt 1 trained all
50 epochs then crashed at evaluation with `KeyError: 'f1'` in the lens block, a path only the
combined mode reaches. The worker patched the key, proved the trainer md5 changed, and relaunched;
attempt 2 finished at 0.6380 object macro AP and 0.8815 lens judgeable macro AP.

**034 verify round 3.** 361 comparisons, November's reference re-derived from its own scores, and the
count correction: nineteen trained columns, not twenty.

**035 pass/fail and workbook.** At 0.5: 760 of 768 fail pictures caught, 1 clear picture wrongly
failed, 8 of 36 bubble-only pictures passed, 324 of 353 passed overall. Workbook written with seven
sheets and independently re-checked by the operator against the saved scores.

**036 documentation sweep.** Reclaimed from its worker after 77 minutes with none of its four
outputs written; finished by the operator.

## Incidents worth remembering

- The `sudo` tee-over-ssh push auto-approves interactively and is refused in a worker's single-query
  mode. It cost 20 minutes on 028 and the same guard refused `execute_code` on 034.
- 028 ended a turn without a terminal call and sat `running` for 15 minutes. That produced the limbo
  detector now in the orchestrator skill.
- 033's crash cost a full 50-epoch run because the smoke never exercised the evaluation branch.
- A verifier correctly caught a round card claiming five artifacts when the checkpoint was remote
  only, then pulled 181 MB into a tracked directory. Weights are now gitignored under `run/`.

## What is open

- Bubble-only pictures fail 28 of 36, driven by Foreign Matter (23) and Fiber (11). Hard negatives or
  a specificity term is the first queued experiment.
- Cuts at 0.95 for HEMA and No Lens sit on the grid boundary; the grid needs to reach 0.99.
- One seed only. Deltas under about 0.03 AP are unproven; a second seed is the cheapest error bar.
- The gate-plus-weight arm was never run, so "the gate is worse" is established against an
  unweighted gate, not against the best gate.
